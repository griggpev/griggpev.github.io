Bio

I was born in Las Vegas, Nevada and lived briefly in Nederland, Colorado before finally moving to Raritan, New Jersey where I'd spend the next 20 years or so. Growing up, math never really interested me as I much preferred MMOs and old movies to schoolwork. In fact, my very low grades throughout all of primary, middle, and highschool reflected that—I got into a lot of trouble and nearly dropped out a few times. When I somehow floundered my way into an AP calculus course my senior year (despite having neither any desire nor plan to go to college), my then-teacher, Dr. (Steve) Kane, believed I was capable of more. Being seen supplied me with reasons to try. That one could so easily be amenable to change merely by being seen was—and still remains to me today—the most important lesson I had ever learned.

I was a first-generation college student starting at my local community college. After two years, I had the option of joining my friends in transferring to Rutgers or NJIT to earn my bachelor's, but my family's path led west to Oregon. Coincidentally, I had recently heard about an alternative liberal arts school in Portland called Reed College, and so the list of things pulling me westward kept growing. With a letter from Doc Kane, I would end up attending Reed 3,000 miles away from the life I'd had before. The intensity of Reed's liberal arts education shaped who I am today. I came to understand math as an intrinsically humane and communal endeavor, not unlike storytelling or music.
