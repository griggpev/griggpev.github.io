ea-hwtp.sty. Standard homework template with usual theorem environments and unnumbered variants. Exercise and solution environments are the bread and butter for the student. Document information is controlled with the name, course, homework, and date commands. Download .sty·View source

ea-notes.sty. Lecture-notes layout, with sans-serif headers, ruled section breaks, and boxed theorem, definition, and question environments. Styling is modeled in large part on Jim Belk’s fantastic notes, which are well worth a look on their own. Comes with Open color on hand for whatever a given page needs, and cleveref already hooked up for the environments. Download .sty·View source
